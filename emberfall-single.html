Emberfall

A pocket-sized MMO realm: a village, a haunted wood, a crypt, a mine of living crystal, a sea of islands and isles adrift in the sky. A main story, dozens of side quests, six professions, talents, mounts and five great bosses. Plays with mouse and keys, a controller, or two thumbs.

Choose a calling

Controls

Move
Put your thumb anywhere on the left half of the screen and drag. Or tap the ground to walk there; you'll path around obstacles
Target
Tap an enemy, or the crosshair button to cycle through nearby enemies
Attack
Tap your target again, or press any ability. With no target, abilities pick the nearest enemy and walk you into range
Abilities
The ring of round buttons. Hold one to read what it does. The outer three unlock as you level
Potions
The red and blue buttons at the ends of the ring
Action
The gold button appears beside people, herbs, ore, fishing spots, pages and portals
Map
Tap the minimap to enlarge it. Tap the chat lines for the full log
Move
WASD or the arrow keys, or click the ground
Target
Click an enemy, or Tab to cycle
Attack
Click your target again, or right-click it. Abilities on 1 to 7
Potions
Q for health, E for mana or energy
Interact
F talks to, gathers from or uses whatever is nearest. Or click it
Panels
B pack, L quests, P professions, T talents, H controls, Esc closes
Other
R Return Rune, M mount, 8 novelty ability. Change any key in Settings. A game controller works too: left stick moves, face buttons and bumpers are abilities

Tips: casting stops if you move. Enemies give up if you run far enough. Bosses mark the ground before they strike it, so step out of the red. People with a gold ! have work for you; a gold ? means you've finished their task.
